What it does

Bring an email you feel unsure about, or a hard conversation, social post, big purchase, work call, or family advice.

  • It reviews drafts before you send them.
  • Your other Grok bots can consult it before sends, posts, purchases, deletions, or hard-to-undo actions.
  • An optional daily evening reflection stays off until you turn it on. It runs in your own local time.

Where its principles come from

Second Opinion draws on timeless teachings in the Bahá'í writings: honesty, fairness, kindness, and consultation. Passages appear only verbatim, always with sources, and they stay optional. Anyone can use it, whatever they believe.
